General inspection and safe-use principles

Before lifting

  • Verify identification, WLL/SWL markings and traceability as applicable.
  • Check compatibility with the load, lifting points and other rigging components.
  • Inspect for damage, wear, deformation, corrosion, cuts and heat damage.
  • Confirm the load's weight, centre of gravity and planned sling arrangement.

During operations

  • Use only equipment suitable for the load and lifting configuration.
  • Account for sling angles, hitch type, edge protection and load distribution.
  • Keep people clear of suspended loads and line-of-fire hazards.
  • Stop work if the rigging arrangement or equipment condition is uncertain.
Important: These are training notes, not a substitute for a competent person's inspection, the manufacturer's instructions, an approved lift plan, or applicable legal requirements. Remove defective equipment from service and quarantine it pending assessment.

Standards note: TÜV is a group of independent technical inspection and certification organisations, not one universal lifting-equipment standard. LEEA provides industry guidance, training and technical publications. Always confirm the applicable standard and edition for the equipment and jurisdiction.
