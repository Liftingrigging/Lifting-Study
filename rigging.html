Study notes for lifting and rigging operations

1. Wire Rope Slings

Wire rope slings are commonly used for lifting loads. Study their construction, identification, rated capacity, inspection requirements and correct configurations.

2. Web Slings

Web slings are made from synthetic materials and are used for many lifting applications. Study sling types, configurations, inspection and limitations.

3. Shackles

Shackles connect lifting equipment and loads. Common types include bow shackles and dee shackles. Study markings, rated capacity, inspection and proper use.

4. Lifting Hooks

Study hook types, identification markings, safety latches, inspection requirements and correct connection methods.

5. Master Links

Master links are used as connecting components in many sling assemblies. Study their identification, rated capacity and inspection requirements.

6. Spreader Beams

Spreader beams can help distribute lifting forces. Their use must follow the manufacturer's information and the approved engineering or lifting plan.

7. Lifting Clamps

Lifting clamps are designed for specific applications. Study the manufacturer's instructions, rated capacity, inspection requirements and limitations.

8. Inspection

Lifting equipment should be inspected according to applicable regulations, standards, manufacturer instructions and site procedures. Damaged or questionable equipment should not be used until properly assessed.

Important Safety Notice

These pages are for study and reference only. Always follow the approved lift plan, manufacturer instructions, applicable standards and site procedures. Lifting operations must be planned and verified by appropriately competent personnel.
