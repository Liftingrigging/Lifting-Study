Lifting Accessories Study Reference

Lifting accessories are equipment and components used to connect, support, position or control loads during lifting operations.

This page is for education and study. Actual lifting equipment must be selected and used according to manufacturer information, applicable requirements and an approved lifting plan.

1. Wire Rope Slings

Wire Rope Slings

Wire rope slings are flexible lifting assemblies made from wire rope with suitable end terminations.

Common Types

  • Single-leg wire rope sling
  • Two-leg sling
  • Three-leg sling
  • Four-leg sling
  • Endless wire rope sling
  • Grommet sling

Study Points

  • Wire rope construction
  • Diameter
  • End terminations
  • Identification and capacity information
  • Sling configuration
  • Application limitations

2. Webbing Slings

Synthetic Webbing Slings

Webbing slings are flexible synthetic lifting accessories commonly used where a soft contact surface is desirable.

Common Configurations

  • Vertical hitch
  • Basket configuration
  • Choker configuration
  • Endless sling

Study Points

  • Material identification
  • Width and length
  • Rated capacity information
  • Configuration effects
  • Edge protection requirements

3. Round Slings

Round Slings

Round slings are flexible synthetic lifting accessories consisting of load-bearing fibers enclosed in a protective cover.

  • Endless construction
  • Flexible load connection
  • Various rated capacities
  • Different lengths available
  • Configuration affects capacity

4. Chain Slings

Alloy Steel Chain Slings

Chain slings are assemblies using lifting-grade chain and compatible components.

Common Assemblies

  • Single-leg
  • Two-leg
  • Three-leg
  • Four-leg

Components

  • Chain
  • Master link
  • Connecting links
  • Hooks
  • Shortening components

5. Shackles

Shackles

Shackles are connecting components used to connect lifting accessories to loads, lifting points and other equipment.

Common Types

  • Bow shackles
  • Dee shackles
  • Screw-pin shackles
  • Safety-pin shackles
  • Special-purpose shackles

Study Points

  • Shackle body
  • Pin
  • Identification markings
  • Rated capacity
  • Correct configuration

6. Lifting Hooks

Lifting Hooks

Hooks provide a connection between lifting equipment and the load or lifting accessory.

  • Eye hooks
  • Clevis hooks
  • Swivel hooks
  • Self-locking hooks
  • Foundry hooks
  • Grab hooks

Study Points

  • Hook identification
  • Rated capacity
  • Hook throat
  • Safety latch
  • Connection arrangement

7. Master Links & Connecting Links

Master Links

Master links provide the main connection point for multi-leg chain assemblies and other lifting arrangements.

  • Master link
  • Master link assembly
  • Sub-assemblies
  • Connecting links

8. Lifting Points

Swivel Hoist Rings & Lifting Points

Engineered lifting points provide designated connection locations for lifting loads.

  • Fixed lifting eyes
  • Swivel lifting points
  • Hoist rings
  • Weld-on lifting points
  • Bolt-on lifting points

9. Spreader Beams

Spreader Beams

Spreader beams are lifting devices designed to distribute lifting connections and manage the geometry of a load.

  • Beam body
  • Top connection
  • Bottom lifting points
  • Rated capacity
  • Design configuration

10. Lifting Beams

Lifting Beams

Lifting beams are below-the-hook devices designed for specific lifting arrangements and load configurations.

  • Single-point lifting beams
  • Multi-point lifting beams
  • Adjustable lifting beams
  • Special-purpose lifting beams

11. Lifting Frames

Lifting Frames

Lifting frames are engineered structures used to support and connect loads during lifting.

  • Modular lifting frames
  • Equipment lifting frames
  • Pipe lifting frames
  • Special-purpose frames

12. Lifting Clamps

Plate Lifting Clamps

Plate clamps are designed for lifting specific types of plate or steel products according to their manufacturer's requirements.

  • Vertical plate clamps
  • Horizontal plate clamps
  • Universal plate clamps
  • Beam clamps
  • Rail clamps

13. Lifting Magnets

Magnetic Lifters

Magnetic lifting devices use magnetic force to handle suitable ferromagnetic materials.

  • Permanent magnetic lifters
  • Electromagnetic lifting devices
  • Magnetic plate lifters

14. Vacuum Lifters

Vacuum Lifters

Vacuum lifting devices use suction or vacuum systems to handle suitable loads.

  • Glass lifters
  • Panel lifters
  • Vacuum lifting frames
  • Powered vacuum lifters

15. Drum Lifters

Drum Lifters

Drum lifting devices are designed for handling drums and similar containers.

  • Vertical drum lifters
  • Horizontal drum lifters
  • Drum clamps

16. Pipe Lifters

Pipe Lifting Equipment

Pipe lifting devices are designed for handling pipes and cylindrical loads using equipment suitable for the specific application.

  • Pipe hooks
  • Pipe clamps
  • Pipe lifting beams
  • Special pipe lifting devices

17. Pallet Lifters

Pallet Lifters

Pallet lifting devices are used for handling compatible pallets and materials.

  • Adjustable pallet forks
  • Fixed pallet forks
  • Construction pallet lifters

18. Turnbuckles

Turnbuckles

Turnbuckles are mechanical tensioning devices used in suitable rigging and structural applications.

  • Eye-and-eye
  • Jaw-and-jaw
  • Eye-and-jaw
  • Open-body types

19. Chain Shorteners

Chain Shortening Devices

Chain shortening devices are used in compatible chain sling assemblies to establish the required effective leg length.

  • Shortening hooks
  • Shortening clutches
  • Chain shortening devices

20. Wire Rope Accessories

Wire Rope Thimbles

Thimbles provide a protective bearing surface at wire rope eyes and help maintain the intended eye shape.

Wire Rope Clips

Wire rope clips are mechanical fittings used in certain wire rope terminations where permitted by the applicable requirements.

Wire Rope Grips

Wire rope grips and related devices are used for specific wire rope handling and connection applications.

21. Snatch Blocks & Sheaves

Snatch Blocks

Snatch blocks contain a sheave and provide a means of changing or redirecting wire rope movement in compatible lifting arrangements.

  • Single-sheave blocks
  • Multiple-sheave blocks
  • Side-opening snatch blocks
  • Special-purpose blocks

22. Concrete Lifting Accessories

Concrete Lifting Anchors

Concrete lifting anchors are embedded or connected lifting systems designed for compatible precast concrete products.

Lifting Clutches

Lifting clutches connect compatible lifting anchors to lifting equipment during handling of designed concrete products.

23. Container Lifting Equipment

Container Lifting Devices

Container lifting equipment is designed for compatible shipping containers and must match the container connection arrangement.

  • Container lifting frames
  • Container spreaders
  • Corner fitting lifting devices
  • Special container lifting systems

24. Below-the-Hook Devices

Below-the-Hook Lifting Devices

Below-the-hook devices are engineered devices positioned between the crane hook and the load.

  • Lifting beams
  • Spreader beams
  • Lifting frames
  • Magnets
  • Vacuum lifters
  • Clamps
  • Special lifting devices

25. Accessory Identification

Identification & Capacity Information

Lifting accessories should have appropriate identification and capacity information as required by the applicable equipment, manufacturer and regulatory requirements.

  • Manufacturer
  • Identification number
  • Rated capacity information
  • Size or dimensions
  • Material or grade where applicable
  • Applicable configuration information

Study Note

The correct lifting accessory depends on the load, connection points, load geometry, environment, equipment compatibility and approved lifting arrangement. Always use manufacturer information and the applicable requirements for the specific equipment.
