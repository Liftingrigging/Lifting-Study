Lifting Safety Study

Safety topics for lifting and rigging operations

← Back to Home

1. Suspended Loads

Never place people under a suspended load unless a specific approved procedure and applicable requirements permit it. Keep personnel away from hazardous areas around lifting operations.

2. Dropped Objects

Dropped objects can cause serious injuries and damage. Controls should consider load security, rigging condition, exclusion zones and the surrounding work area.

3. Exclusion Zones

Establish a suitable controlled area around lifting activities. Unauthorized personnel should be prevented from entering hazardous areas.

4. Pre-Use Inspection

Lifting equipment should be checked before use according to applicable requirements. Look for damage, deformation, excessive wear, missing identification or other defects.

5. Weather and Wind

Wind, rain, lightning, poor visibility and other weather conditions can affect lifting operations. Follow the crane manufacturer's limits and site procedures.

6. Communication

The lifting team should agree on communication methods before starting. Signals and radio communication should be clear and understood by everyone involved.

7. Banksman and Rigger

Responsibilities should be clearly assigned to competent personnel. The lifting team should understand who controls signals, rigging activities and communication with the operator.

8. Emergency Planning

Lifting plans should consider foreseeable emergencies, including equipment failure, loss of communication, unexpected movement and changing site conditions.

9. Stop Work

If conditions become unsafe or the lift no longer matches the approved plan, the operation should be stopped and reassessed by the responsible personnel.

10. Safety Checklist

  • Approved lift plan available
  • Load information verified
  • Rigging equipment suitable and inspected
  • Crane configuration verified
  • Communication method agreed
  • Exclusion zone established
  • Weather conditions checked
  • Personnel responsibilities understood
Important Safety Notice

This page is for study and reference only. Always follow applicable laws, regulations, standards, manufacturer instructions, company procedures and approved lift plans. Actual lifting operations require appropriately competent personnel and site-specific controls.
