Calculation tools

Calculate each topic separately. Results are estimates and must be checked against the actual lift configuration and approved engineering documents.

1. Crane Geometry

Calculate a missing boom length, horizontal radius, vertical tip rise or boom angle.

Idealised right triangle: radius = L cos θ; rise = L sin θ. Actual crane geometry, boom deflection and hook block position may differ.

2. Sling-Leg Tension

Estimate equal load sharing across symmetric sling legs.

Idealised tension per leg = W ÷ (n sin θ). This assumes equal sharing and a symmetric arrangement. Real sling forces may be higher.

3. Load Weight from Dimensions

Estimate a uniform rectangular solid's mass from dimensions and density.

Mass = volume × density + additional mass. Use actual drawings, weigh records or manufacturer data whenever available.

4. Load Sharing

Estimate reactions for a simply supported beam with a point load.

For an ideal simply supported beam: Rleft = P(L − x)/L; Rright = Px/L. Not valid for arbitrary multi-point rigging or flexible loads.

5. Centre of Gravity — Two Masses

Calculate the centre of gravity along one axis for two known masses.

xCG = (m₁x₁ + m₂x₂) ÷ (m₁ + m₂). Use a common datum for both positions.

6. Ground Bearing Pressure

Estimate average pressure from a known load and effective support area.

Average pressure = load ÷ area. Actual outrigger reactions and soil bearing capacity require separate verification.

7. Obstacle Clearance

Estimate geometric clearance for a boom represented by a straight line.

Assumes a straight, rigid boom line. Does not model boom deflection, jib geometry, hook block, suspended load swing or crane motion.

8. Crane Capacity Utilisation

Compare the total planned load with the applicable chart capacity.

Utilisation = planned chart load ÷ rated chart capacity × 100%. Ensure the load basis and deductions match the crane manufacturer's chart.

Engineering checks before using a result

Important: These tools are simplified estimates, not a substitute for an approved lift plan or a qualified lifting engineer's review.
  • Confirm actual load weight, centre of gravity, lifting points and rigging weights.
  • Use the exact crane model, configuration, radius, boom/jib arrangement and load chart.
  • Account for deductions, reeving, wind, dynamic effects, side loading and manufacturer limits.
  • Verify sling angles, hitch factors, hardware WLL, load distribution and edge protection.
  • Check ground conditions, outrigger reactions, mats and underground services.
  • Confirm clearances along the entire lifting path, not just at the boom tip.
