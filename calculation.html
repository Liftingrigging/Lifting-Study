Important Safety Note

These calculators provide simplified geometric estimates only. They do not establish crane capacity or prove that the complete boom envelope clears a building. Actual boom configuration, deflection, jib arrangement, crane orientation, and load movement may affect clearances.

Verify the full lift path, crane capacity, and configuration using the manufacturer's documents and an approved lift plan reviewed by a competent lifting professional.
