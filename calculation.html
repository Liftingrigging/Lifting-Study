Saudi Aramco Reference Section

This section is dedicated to the study of lifting and rigging requirements associated with Saudi Aramco work, including rigging hardware, crane lifts, inspection, testing, certification, lifting calculations and power-line hazards.

The material is organized around applicable Saudi Aramco General Instructions and related lifting standards.

IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTICE

This website is intended for education, study and reference only. It is not a substitute for an approved lifting plan, engineered calculation, manufacturer load chart, equipment certificate, current Saudi Aramco requirements, applicable Saudi regulations, applicable ASME standards, or verification by a competent person.

Always verify the current revision of the applicable document before using any requirement for an actual lifting operation.

Core Lifting Calculation Reference

1. Load Weight

Weight is calculated from mass and gravitational acceleration.

W = m × g
  • W = weight / force in newtons
  • m = mass in kilograms
  • g ≈ 9.81 m/s²

2. Metric Tonnes

Tonnes = Kilograms ÷ 1000

Example: 5,000 kg = 5 tonnes.

3. Load Moment

M = W × R
  • M = moment
  • W = load
  • R = horizontal working radius

For study purposes, when load is expressed in tonnes and radius in metres, the result may be expressed as tonne-metres.

4. Two-Leg Sling Tension

For a simplified symmetrical two-leg sling arrangement, with the sling angle measured from the horizontal:

T = W ÷ (2 × sin θ)
  • T = tension in each sling leg
  • W = total supported load
  • θ = sling angle from horizontal

Actual sling capacity must always be checked against the applicable manufacturer data and approved lifting arrangement.

5. D/d Ratio

D/d = D ÷ d
  • D = diameter of curvature around the support
  • d = sling or wire-rope diameter

The applicable capacity or reduction requirement must be taken from the applicable manufacturer documentation or governing requirement. The D/d ratio by itself must not be used to invent a capacity reduction factor.

6. Ground Bearing Pressure

P = F ÷ A
  • P = pressure
  • F = applied force
  • A = bearing area

Actual crane outrigger reactions must come from the crane manufacturer, approved engineering calculation or applicable lift plan. Do not simply divide total crane weight equally between outriggers.

7. Required Bearing Area

A = F ÷ Pallow

Pallow must be based on an appropriate site-specific geotechnical or engineering assessment.

8. Static Equilibrium

ΣFx = 0 ΣFy = 0 ΣM = 0

These relationships are fundamental to determining forces, reactions and load distribution in lifting arrangements.

9. Gross Suspended Load

Gross Load = Payload + Rigging + Lifting Device + Other Suspended Items

Crane capacity checks must account for the applicable suspended components, not only the payload.

10. Crane Utilization

Utilization (%) = Actual Load ÷ Applicable Crane Capacity × 100

The applicable capacity must be obtained from the correct crane load chart for the actual crane configuration, radius, boom, counterweight, outrigger condition and other applicable parameters.
