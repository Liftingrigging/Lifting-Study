Quick Formula Reference

Load Weight

W = m × g

W = weight in newtons
m = mass in kilograms
g = gravitational acceleration

Moment

M = W × R

M = moment
W = load force
R = horizontal radius

Ground Pressure

P = F ÷ A

P = pressure
F = applied force
A = supporting area

Required Support Area

A = F ÷ Pallow

A = required area
F = applied force
Pallow = allowable ground pressure

Two-Leg Sling Tension

T = W ÷ (2 × sin θ)

θ is measured from the horizontal for this formula.

Center of Gravity

x = Σ(W × x) ÷ ΣW

Useful for estimating the load balance point in a simple one-dimensional case.
