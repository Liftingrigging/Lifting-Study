Safety & Engineering Notice

These calculators are provided for study, learning and preliminary calculation purposes only.

Calculator results do not replace an approved lift plan, manufacturer load chart, certified equipment data, engineering calculations, applicable standards or competent-person verification.

Actual crane capacity, rigging WLL, ground conditions, structural capacity and lifting arrangements must be verified before any lifting operation.
