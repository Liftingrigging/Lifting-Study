Quick Formula Reference

Load Weight

W = m × g

Weight is related to mass and gravitational acceleration.

Load Moment

M = W × R

Moment depends on load and horizontal radius.

Two-Leg Sling Tension

T = W ÷ (2 × sin θ)

Simplified symmetrical arrangement with the angle measured from the horizontal.

D/d Ratio

D/d = D ÷ d

D is the curvature diameter and d is the applicable sling or wire-rope diameter.

Ground Bearing Pressure

P = F ÷ A

Pressure equals applied force divided by bearing area.
