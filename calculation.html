Lifting & Rigging Calculators

This section provides calculation tools for lifting, rigging, crane operations and study purposes.

The calculators are intended as educational and reference tools. Actual lifting operations must be verified against approved lift plans, manufacturer data, applicable standards, site procedures and competent-person requirements.

Important Safety Notice

These calculators are provided for study and reference purposes only.

A calculation result does not constitute an approved lift plan or engineering approval.

For actual lifting operations, verify the specific crane configuration, manufacturer's load chart, lifting accessories, WLL/SWL, ground conditions, environmental conditions, load geometry and applicable requirements.

Always follow the approved lift plan, manufacturer instructions, applicable standards and site procedures and obtain competent-person verification where required.
