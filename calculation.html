Quick Formula Reference

1. Weight

W = m × g
  • W = weight / force
  • m = mass
  • g ≈ 9.81 m/s²

2. Two-Leg Sling Tension

T = W ÷ (2 × sin θ)
  • T = tension in each sling leg
  • W = supported load
  • θ = sling angle measured from horizontal

3. Load Moment

M = W × R
  • M = load moment
  • W = load
  • R = working radius

4. D/d Ratio

D/d = D ÷ d
  • D = diameter of curvature
  • d = sling or wire-rope diameter

5. Ground Bearing Pressure

P = F ÷ A
  • P = pressure
  • F = applied force
  • A = bearing area

6. Required Bearing Area

A = F ÷ Pallow

The allowable ground pressure must be based on the appropriate engineering or geotechnical assessment.

7. Static Equilibrium

ΣFx = 0 ΣFy = 0 ΣM = 0

8. Gross Suspended Load

Gross Load = Payload + Rigging + Lifting Device + Other Suspended Items
