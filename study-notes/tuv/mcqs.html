TÜV Lifting & Rigging Practice Questions

These questions are designed as study material for lifting, rigging, crane and signalman topics.

They are educational practice questions and are not a replacement for approved training, certification requirements, manufacturer instructions, lift plans, regulations or competent-person verification.

Topic: Pre-use Inspection

1. What should a rigger do before using lifting accessories?

Correct Answer: B
A pre-use inspection should be completed before lifting equipment and accessories are used.

Topic: SWL

2. What does SWL mean?

Correct Answer: A
SWL means Safe Working Load. The actual permitted capacity must be verified from the applicable equipment identification, manufacturer information and requirements.

Topic: Lifting Accessories

3. Which of the following is commonly used to connect a sling to a lifting point?

Correct Answer: A
Shackles are common lifting accessories used for connecting compatible lifting components.

Topic: Sling Angle

4. What generally happens to sling-leg tension as the sling angle from horizontal becomes smaller?

Correct Answer: B
For a simplified symmetric sling arrangement, reducing the angle from horizontal increases the tension in the sling legs.

Topic: Load Weight

5. Why should the rigger verify the load weight before lifting?

Correct Answer: A
Knowing the load weight is important when selecting lifting equipment and planning a safe lifting operation.

Topic: Center of Gravity

6. Why is the center of gravity important during lifting?

Correct Answer: A
The center of gravity affects how a load balances and how lifting points and rigging should be arranged.

Topic: Hand Signals

7. What is the purpose of standard crane hand signals?

Correct Answer: A
Standard signals provide a recognized method of communication between the signal person and crane operator.

Topic: Load Control

8. What is one important objective when controlling a suspended load?

Correct Answer: A
TÜV lifting and rigging training includes controlling loads and minimizing swing as an important safety topic.

Topic: Ground Conditions

9. Why are ground conditions important when setting up a crane?

Correct Answer: A
Ground conditions can affect crane stability and support, especially when outriggers are used.

Topic: Outriggers

10. What should be considered before deploying crane outriggers?

Correct Answer: A
Ground condition and adequate support are important considerations when setting up outriggers.

Topic: Lifting Hazards

11. What should a rigger do when identifying a serious lifting hazard?

Correct Answer: B
TÜV's rigger competency material includes communicating hazards to the crane operator and taking appropriate action.

Topic: Lifting Accessories

12. What should be checked regarding lifting accessories before use?

Correct Answer: A
The accessory should be inspected for obvious defects and its required certification or identification should be current and applicable.

Topic: Critical Lift

13. What is important when preparing for a critical lift?

Correct Answer: A
Critical lifts require appropriate planning, controls and coordination according to the applicable site procedure and lift plan.

Topic: Travel Path

14. What should a rigger consider before moving a suspended load?

Correct Answer: A
The load travel path should be identified and checked for hazards before moving the load.

Topic: Environmental Conditions

15. Which environmental condition can affect a lifting operation?

Correct Answer: D
Environmental conditions such as wind, visibility, fog, rain and heat can affect lifting safety and must be considered.

Topic: Exclusion Zones

16. Why are barriers or exclusion zones used during lifting?

Correct Answer: A
Barriers and exclusion zones help prevent unauthorized personnel from entering hazardous areas around lifting operations.

Topic: Rigging Hitches

17. What should a rigger understand about rigging hitches?

Correct Answer: A
Correct hitch selection and application are important parts of safe rigging and load attachment.

Topic: Lifting Points

18. Why should designated lifting points be identified?

Correct Answer: A
Lifting points should be identified and verified as suitable for the intended lifting arrangement.

Topic: Responsibilities

19. Which group has responsibilities in a lifting operation?

Correct Answer: D
TÜV training identifies operator, rigger and supervisory responsibilities as part of lifting safety.

Topic: Lift Planning

20. What is a fundamental part of preparing for a lift?

Correct Answer: A
Proper planning is fundamental to safe lifting. The applicable approved lift plan, site procedure and competent-person requirements must be followed.
Important Safety Notice

These MCQs are for study and revision only. They do not provide authorization to perform lifting operations.

Always follow the approved lift plan, manufacturer instructions, applicable standards and regulations, site procedures, and competent-person requirements.
