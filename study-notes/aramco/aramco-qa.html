General Lifting Safety

Choose a question to reveal its answer.

1. What should be checked before a lifting operation?

Check the approved lift plan, load information, lifting equipment, operator and crew qualifications, communication arrangements, and site hazards. Follow the applicable site procedure.

2. What does WLL mean?

WLL means Working Load Limit. Never exceed the applicable rated capacity of the lifting gear or equipment.

3. What should you do if lifting gear is damaged?

Stop using it, prevent further use, and report it according to site procedures. It must be assessed by an authorized person before any return to service.

4. What is the role of a designated signal person?

The signal person communicates agreed signals to the crane operator and helps coordinate the lift. The crew must understand the signals and communication method before lifting begins.

5. What is a suspended-load hazard?

A suspended load can fall, swing, or shift unexpectedly. Keep people clear of the danger area and never stand or walk beneath a suspended load.

6. Why is a lift plan important?

It identifies the load, equipment, roles, lifting method, hazards, controls, and communication arrangements needed for the planned operation.

7. What should happen if conditions become unsafe?

Stop the operation when safe to do so, keep people clear, communicate the hazard, and resume only after the issue has been addressed and the required authorization obtained.

8. Why is the load weight important?

The load weight is needed to select suitable equipment and verify that the planned configuration and capacities are adequate. Account for relevant rigging accessories and attachments.

9. What is the purpose of a pre-lift briefing?

It ensures the team understands the plan, individual roles, hazards, exclusion zones, signals, and actions to take if the lift must be stopped.

10. Can damaged or unidentified lifting accessories be used?

No. Remove them from service and report them. Follow the applicable inspection and approval process before any equipment is reused.
