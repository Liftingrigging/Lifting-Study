Calculation basis and limits

R_A = W × x / L
R_B = W × (L − x) / L
R_A + R_B = W

W = total lifted weight; L = distance between the two lifting points; x = distance from tail point B to the centre of gravity. For two vertical reactions, the horizontal moment arms contain the same cos(angle) factor, which cancels from the moment equation. Consequently, these reactions are constant in this model.

Do not use this simplified model to authorize a lift. Actual tailing operations may have sling angles, non-vertical hoist lines, moving hook coordinates, ground reactions, load transfer during pick-up, and dynamic effects. These can change the force distribution. To calculate those loads, a competent lifting engineer must define the actual geometry at each stage and solve force and moment equilibrium for that configuration. Verify every stage against certified crane load charts and the approved lift plan. This calculator is not certified by TÜV NORD or LEEA.
