Tailing Load Distribution Calculator

Two-Point Tailing / Equilibrium Study Tool

Input Data

Study Model

Total Length = A + B
Main Lift Reaction = W × B ÷ (A + B)
Tailing Reaction = W × A ÷ (A + B)

Where:

  • W = total load
  • A = distance from main lifting point to COG
  • B = distance from COG to tailing point
  • Main Lift Reaction = ideal vertical reaction at the main lift point
  • Tailing Reaction = ideal vertical reaction at the tailing point

Example Study Calculation

Total Load = 20 t A = 6 m B = 4 m Total Length = 10 m

Main Lift Reaction: 20 × 4 ÷ 10 = 8 t

Tailing Reaction: 20 × 6 ÷ 10 = 12 t

Understanding the Result

The load distribution changes as the COG moves relative to the two lifting points.

The point closer to the COG generally carries the larger share of the vertical reaction in this idealized two-support equilibrium model.

Study Note:

This calculator represents an idealized static two-point equilibrium model. Actual tailing operations can involve changing geometry, sling angles, crane motions, acceleration, friction, dynamic effects, unequal rigging and other factors.
Important Safety Notice

This calculator is for study purposes only and does not approve a tailing lift.

Actual tailing operations require an engineered or approved lift plan addressing crane configuration, load geometry, COG, lifting points, rigging, sling angles, ground conditions, clearances and communication.

Do not assume the calculated vertical reaction is the actual crane or rigging load when the system is moving or when the geometry differs from the simplified model.

Always follow the approved lift plan, manufacturer's instructions and competent-person requirements.
