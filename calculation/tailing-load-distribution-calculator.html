Model used in this tool
Initial horizontal load share is calculated from the centre of gravity. During rotation, the tail-crane share is estimated using a cosine reduction and the head-crane share is the remaining load. At 90°, Crane A carries 100% and Crane B carries 0% in this simplified model.

Important lifting safety note: This is a preliminary illustrative calculator, not a certified lift-plan tool. The cosine transfer model is an assumption, not a universal LEEA or TÜV NORD formula. Actual tailing-crane loads must be calculated for the specific load, lifting lugs, sling arrangement, hook trajectories, support conditions and operating sequence. A competent lifting engineer must verify the calculations and account for applicable dynamic effects and required capacity margins before the lift is performed.
Use consistent units. The calculated values are load forces expressed as equivalent tonnes, not crane-capacity ratings. This tool does not select cranes or confirm that either crane is suitable for the operation.
