This version models an ideal rigid item supported by two vertical forces at its two lifting points. Under these assumptions, the static reaction loads do not change with angle: the horizontal moment arms both scale with cos(angle). Real tailing lifts can have changing forces due to sling angles, lifting-point geometry, crane motion, ground contact, reeving and other effects. This model does not calculate those effects.

Calculation basis

Crane A reaction = W × x / L
Crane B reaction = W × (L − x) / L

W = total lifted weight; L = distance between lifting points; x = distance from tailing point B to the centre of gravity. The two reactions add up to W.

Safety limitation: This is a simplified static-equilibrium calculator, not a TÜV NORD or LEEA certified calculation. Before lifting, a competent lifting engineer must verify the actual rigging geometry, sling-force components, lifting points, crane movements, load-chart capacities, ground conditions, dynamic effects and the approved lift plan. Do not use this output alone to approve or execute a lift.
