Calculate Angle Factor

Enter the sling angle measured from the horizontal. The calculator determines the angle factor used in the simplified two-leg symmetrical sling calculation.

Angle Factor = 1 ÷ sin(θ)

For a symmetrical two-leg sling:
Tension per leg = W × Angle Factor ÷ 2
Enter a sling angle and click Calculate.
📚 Important Concept

As the sling becomes flatter, the tension in each sling leg increases. This is why sling angle is an important part of lifting and rigging calculations.
