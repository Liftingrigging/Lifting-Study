Safety-critical limitation
The design load shown is a preliminary screening calculation, not the crane's required rated capacity. It does not prove that a crane can perform the lift. Include hook block, rigging and other applicable deductions as required by the manufacturer's load chart and approved lift plan. Verify the capacity at the actual working radius and required hook height.
Crane recommendation: A specific crane model or tonnage cannot be reliably selected from these four values alone. Confirm the crane configuration, boom length, counterweight, outrigger setup, ground conditions, load-chart capacity and all applicable site and lifting requirements before approving the lift.
🛡

Safety factor & calculation basis

Transparent assumptions

The current example uses a planning calculation factor of 1.25 (25% above the entered load). This is an illustrative assumption only, not a universal crane-selection safety factor and not a verified TÜV Saudi Arabia rule.

The factor is deliberately kept out of the input form so the calculator retains your four requested fields. A competent lifting engineer must establish the correct factor and load treatment for the applicable standard, lift category and manufacturer's requirements.
