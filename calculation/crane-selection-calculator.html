Crane Selection Study Calculator

Preliminary Crane Capacity and Utilization Study

Lift Information

Calculation Method

Gross Load = Main Load + Rigging + Hook Block + Other Suspended Equipment
Crane Utilization = Gross Load ÷ Rated Crane Capacity × 100

The crane capacity must correspond to the actual operating configuration and radius.

Example Study

Main Load = 20 t Rigging = 0.8 t Hook Block = 1.2 t Other Equipment = 0.5 t

Gross Load: 20 + 0.8 + 1.2 + 0.5 = 22.5 t

Crane Rated Capacity = 30 t

Utilization: 22.5 ÷ 30 × 100 = 75%

Study Result Guide

  • ≤ 75% — Lower study utilization
  • 75–85% — Higher utilization; review carefully
  • 85–100% — Very high utilization; requires detailed verification
  • > 100% — Gross load exceeds entered rated capacity
These percentage bands are only a study-screening aid. They are not a universal crane selection or acceptance criterion.

Important Crane Selection Factors

  • Manufacturer load chart
  • Actual operating radius
  • Boom length and configuration
  • Counterweight configuration
  • Jib configuration
  • Hook block and reeving
  • Ground conditions
  • Outrigger configuration
  • Wind and environmental limits
  • Required clearances
  • Crane setup location
  • Lift path and swing area
Important Safety Notice

This calculator is a preliminary study tool only.

It does NOT select or approve a crane for an actual lift.

Crane capacity must be verified from the specific manufacturer's load chart for the actual configuration, radius, boom length, counterweight, outrigger condition, reeving and other applicable factors.

The entered rated capacity must never be treated as a generic crane capacity.

Actual lifting operations require an approved lift plan, competent-person review and compliance with applicable manufacturer requirements, site procedures and regulations.
