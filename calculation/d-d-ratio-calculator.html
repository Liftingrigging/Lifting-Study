Study Formula

D/d = D ÷ d

Where:

  • D = diameter of the curvature around which the sling body is bent
  • d = nominal diameter of the wire rope or sling

Both values must use the same unit. For example, both can be entered in millimetres.

Example Study Calculation

D = 300 mm d = 20 mm D/d = 300 ÷ 20 D/d = 15

Why D/d Matters

When a wire rope sling bends around a small diameter, the bending condition can affect the sling's performance and allowable capacity.

The D/d ratio is therefore an important consideration when evaluating wire rope sling arrangements.

The applicable minimum ratio and any capacity adjustment must come from the relevant standard, manufacturer or approved engineering requirement for the specific sling.

Important Safety Notice

This calculator only calculates the mathematical D/d ratio. It does not determine sling WLL or establish whether a specific lifting arrangement is acceptable.

Do not apply a generic D/d ratio as a universal capacity factor. Use the manufacturer's sling data and applicable standards for the exact sling and configuration.

Inspect lifting equipment and follow the approved lift plan and competent-person requirements.
