Ground Support Study

Use this calculator to study the relationship between an estimated outrigger reaction, allowable ground pressure and support area.

Formulas

Required Support Area: A(required) = R ÷ Pallow

Mat Area: A(mat) = Length × Width

Actual Bearing Pressure: P(actual) = R ÷ A(mat)

Study Margin: Margin = Pallow − P(actual)

Example

Estimated outrigger reaction = 400 kN

Allowable ground pressure = 250 kN/m²

Required area:

400 ÷ 250 = 1.60 m²

For a 2 m × 2 m mat:

Area = 4.00 m²

Actual bearing pressure:

400 ÷ 4 = 100 kN/m²

Important Safety Notice

This is a simplified study calculation. It does not determine the actual maximum outrigger reaction of a crane or approve the use of any mat, pad or support system.

Actual outrigger reactions must be obtained from the crane manufacturer, approved engineering calculations or the applicable lift plan.

Ground capacity depends on actual soil conditions, depth, moisture, underground structures, voids, slopes, drainage and the complete support arrangement.

Mat strength, stiffness, thickness, material, bending, punching, shear and load distribution also require appropriate engineering verification.
