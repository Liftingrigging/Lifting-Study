Calculation Notes

Important: These formulas calculate the vertical reactions at two sling support points for a static load in equilibrium. They do not directly calculate the actual axial tension in angled sling legs. Actual sling tension depends on sling angle, number of load-bearing legs, rigging configuration, and applicable design factors. Verify the lift plan and rigging arrangement with a qualified lifting professional before use.
