Calculate Combined Center of Gravity

Enter two component weights and their distances from a common reference point. The calculator determines the combined center of gravity along one axis.

x = Σ(W × x) ÷ ΣW

x = Combined Center of Gravity
W = Component Weight
x = Component Position
Enter the component information and calculate.
📚 Why Center of Gravity Matters

The center of gravity affects how a load behaves when it is lifted. If the lifting arrangement does not properly account for the load's center of gravity, the load can tilt, rotate or become unstable.

⚠️ Study Use Only

This calculator is a simplified one-dimensional educational model. Real loads may require two-dimensional or three-dimensional analysis and consideration of actual geometry, lifting points, rigging arrangement and load distribution.

Actual lifting operations require verified engineering calculations and competent-person review.
