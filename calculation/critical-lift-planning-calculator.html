Critical Lift Planning Calculator

Lifting & Rigging Study Tool

Lift Planning Inputs

This tool provides a preliminary numerical study of common lift-planning parameters. It is not a substitute for an approved critical lift plan.

Basic Formulas

Gross Load = Main Load + Rigging + Hook Block + Other Equipment

Crane Utilization (%) = (Gross Load ÷ Rated Capacity) × 100

Load Moment = Gross Load × Operating Radius

Required Ground Bearing Area = Ground Reaction ÷ Allowable Ground Bearing Pressure

Planning Checks

1. Load Identification
Confirm actual load weight, dimensions and center of gravity from reliable information.
2. Crane Configuration
Confirm crane model, boom configuration, counterweight, outriggers, reeving and other applicable configuration details.
3. Load Chart
Confirm the manufacturer's rated capacity at the actual operating radius and configuration.
4. Rigging
Verify sling, shackle, hook, lifting beam and other accessory identification, certification, WLL and configuration.
5. Ground Conditions
Verify ground suitability, outrigger support, mats/pads and actual ground-bearing requirements.
6. Environment
Consider wind, weather, visibility, overhead obstructions, power lines and nearby operations.
7. Communication
Confirm designated signaler/banksman, communication method and emergency arrangements.
8. Exclusion Zone
Establish suitable controls to prevent unauthorized personnel from entering the lifting area.

Example

Main load = 40 t

Rigging = 2 t

Hook block = 5 t

Other suspended equipment = 1 t

Crane rated capacity = 60 t

Operating radius = 20 m

Gross load = 48 t

Utilization = 80%

Load moment = 960 tonne-m

Critical Safety Notice

This calculator is an educational and preliminary planning tool only. A numerical result does not mean that a lift is approved or safe to perform.

Crane capacity must be verified from the specific manufacturer's load chart for the actual crane configuration and operating conditions.

Ground-bearing calculations require competent engineering assessment of the actual ground, outrigger reactions, support system and mats or pads. This simplified tool must not be used to determine final outrigger support requirements.

Critical lifts must follow the applicable company procedure, approved lift plan, manufacturer requirements, applicable standards and competent-person review.
