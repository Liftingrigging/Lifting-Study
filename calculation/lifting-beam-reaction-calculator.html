Study Model

This calculator models a simplified symmetrical lifting beam arrangement where the main lifting point is at the beam centre and two lower load points are equally arranged about the centre.

Total Weight = Suspended Load + Beam Weight

For a symmetrical arrangement: Left Load Point Reaction = Total Weight ÷ 2
Right Load Point Reaction = Total Weight ÷ 2
Important:

The distances are shown for study of beam geometry and moment concepts. For a perfectly symmetrical two-point arrangement, the two lower reactions are equal.

Example Calculation

Suspended Load = 20 t Beam Weight = 1 t

Total Weight: 20 + 1 = 21 t

Left Reaction: 21 ÷ 2 = 10.5 t

Right Reaction: 21 ÷ 2 = 10.5 t

Moment Equilibrium Concept

For an asymmetric lifting arrangement, support reactions are determined from static equilibrium.

ΣF = 0

ΣM = 0

For a two-support beam with a load located between the supports:

R₁ = W × b ÷ (a + b)
R₂ = W × a ÷ (a + b)

Where:

  • W = applied load
  • a = distance from load to support 1
  • b = distance from load to support 2
  • R₁ / R₂ = support reactions

What This Calculator Does Not Determine

  • Lifting beam WLL
  • Structural beam strength
  • Deflection
  • Buckling
  • Weld strength
  • Pin or connection capacity
  • Padeye capacity
  • Shackle capacity
  • Sling WLL
  • Crane capacity
Important Safety Notice

This is a simplified study calculator.

A lifting beam is a structural lifting device and its capacity cannot be established from load distribution alone.

Actual lifting beam design and use requires verification of the beam's certified WLL, structural calculations, connections, lifting points, rigging configuration and manufacturer or engineering requirements.

The actual lift must follow the approved lift plan and competent-person requirements.

Do not use this calculator to approve a lifting beam or determine whether a beam is safe for a specific lift.
