Study Formula

T = W ÷ (N × sin θ)

Where:

  • T = calculated tension/load per effective chain leg
  • W = total suspended load
  • N = number of effective load-sharing legs
  • θ = angle measured from horizontal

The calculation assumes an ideal symmetric arrangement with equal load sharing.

Chain Diameter & Grade

Chain sling capacity cannot safely be determined from chain diameter alone.

The rated WLL depends on factors such as:

  • Chain diameter
  • Chain grade
  • Number of legs
  • Sling angle
  • Hitch configuration
  • Master link and connecting components
  • Manufacturer's rated WLL
  • Applicable standard
  • Condition and inspection status
Important:

Do not use a generic diameter-to-capacity formula to determine the actual WLL of a chain sling. Always use the manufacturer's certified WLL table for the exact chain grade, configuration and application.

Safety & Study Notice

This calculator is intended for lifting and rigging study purposes.

It does not determine the certified WLL of a chain sling.

Actual lifting operations require verification of the complete chain sling assembly, including chain, hooks, master links, connectors and other components.

Follow the approved lift plan, manufacturer documentation, applicable standards, site procedures and competent-person verification.
