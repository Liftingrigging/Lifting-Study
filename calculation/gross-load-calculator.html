Load Components

Study Formula

Gross Load = Main Load + Slings + Shackles + Lifting Beam + Hook Block + Other Suspended Equipment

All weights must use the same unit. This calculator uses tonnes.

Example Study Calculation

Main Load = 20.00 t Slings = 0.25 t Shackles = 0.10 t Lifting Beam = 0.80 t Hook Block = 1.20 t Other Equipment = 0.50 t ---------------------------- Gross Load = 22.85 t

Why Gross Load Matters

Crane capacity checks must consider the load being supported by the crane, not only the weight of the main object.

Depending on the crane configuration and lift arrangement, items such as hook blocks, lifting beams, slings, shackles and other suspended equipment may contribute to the crane load.

Important Safety Notice

This calculator is a study and planning aid only.

The actual load to be considered for a crane lift must be determined from the approved lift plan, crane manufacturer's requirements, lifting arrangement and applicable engineering requirements.

Do not use this calculator to replace a crane load chart, engineered lift calculation or competent-person review.
