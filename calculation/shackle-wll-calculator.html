04 — Standards and safety checks

Compliance status: manufacturer verification required.
This calculator does not certify compliance with Saudi requirements, TÜV certification, EN 13889, or any other standard. Verify the applicable project requirements, shackle certificate, WLL marking, material, pin type, and manufacturer's approved loading directions before use.
Never exceed the stamped WLL. Do not assume a shackle is safe for side loading, multiple-leg loading, shock loading, or a particular angle based only on its diameter. If a manufacturer does not permit a loading condition or provide a capacity for it, treat that condition as unverified and do not use the calculated value for lift planning.
