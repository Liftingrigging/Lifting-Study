Study Formula

T = W ÷ (N × sin θ)

Where:

  • T = calculated load at each load-sharing point
  • W = total load
  • N = number of effective load-sharing points
  • θ = sling angle measured from horizontal

The calculated load is a study value for a symmetric/equal-load arrangement.

Important Shackle Information

Shackle WLL is not determined by a universal mathematical formula. The actual allowable WLL must come from the shackle manufacturer's identification, certification and applicable rating tables.

Shackle type, grade, pin configuration, loading direction, connection geometry and side loading can significantly affect allowable capacity.

Safety Notice:

This calculator is a study aid only. Never use a calculated value to override the manufacturer's rated WLL, certification, load-angle limitations or side-loading requirements. Actual lifting operations must follow the approved lift plan, manufacturer instructions, applicable standards and competent-person verification.
