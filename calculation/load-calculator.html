Calculate Load Weight

Enter the mass of the load in kilograms. The calculator converts the mass into weight in kilonewtons.

W = m × g

W = Load Weight (N)
m = Mass (kg)
g = Gravitational Acceleration (m/s²)
Enter a mass and click Calculate.
⚠️ Study Use Only

This calculator is intended for learning and basic reference. Actual lifting operations require verified load information, approved lift planning, manufacturer data, applicable standards and competent-person review.
