Calculate Sling Leg Tension

This calculator estimates the tension in each leg of a two-leg symmetrical sling arrangement.

T = W ÷ (2 × sin θ)

T = Tension in each sling leg
W = Total load weight
θ = Sling angle measured from the horizontal
Enter the load weight and sling angle.
📐 Angle Reminder

In this calculator, the angle is measured from the horizontal.

A smaller sling angle from the horizontal produces higher tension in the sling legs.

⚠️ Study Use Only

This calculator assumes a simplified, symmetrical two-leg arrangement. Actual lifting systems may be affected by unequal load distribution, center of gravity, sling geometry, connection conditions, hitch configuration, bending, edge contact and equipment limitations.

Do not use this calculator as a substitute for an approved lift plan, manufacturer information, certified equipment capacity or competent-person engineering verification.
