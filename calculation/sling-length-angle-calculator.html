Geometric assumptions: In the uneven-point method, all heights use one common base datum and all legs meet at the entered hook/master-link point. Horizontal offsets are measured from the hook's vertical projection. The simple-load method assumes a symmetric two-leg arrangement and a sling angle measured from the horizontal. These calculations do not determine load sharing or certify lifting-point suitability.
