Sling Length & Angle Calculator

Lifting & Rigging Study Tool

Calculate Sling Geometry

This calculator determines sling length and sling angle from the vertical lift height and horizontal distance from the load connection point to the lifting point.

Formulas

Sling Length: L = √(H² + R²)

Sling Angle from Horizontal: θ = tan⁻¹(H ÷ R)

Sling Angle from Vertical: θv = tan⁻¹(R ÷ H)

Where:
L = Sling length
H = Vertical height
R = Horizontal distance

Example

If the vertical height is 4 m and the horizontal distance is 3 m:

Sling length = √(4² + 3²) = 5 m

Angle from horizontal = tan⁻¹(4 ÷ 3) = 53.13°

Angle from vertical = tan⁻¹(3 ÷ 4) = 36.87°

Important Safety Notice

This is a study calculator for basic sling geometry only. It does not determine sling WLL/SWL, allowable configuration, crane capacity, connection strength, or whether a lift is safe to perform.

Actual lifting operations must follow the approved lift plan, manufacturer information, applicable standards, site procedures, and competent-person verification.
