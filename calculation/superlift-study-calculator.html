Superlift / Crane Capacity Study

Use this calculator to study the relationship between gross suspended load and the crane's rated capacity for a selected configuration.

Study Formulas

Gross Load = Main Load + Rigging + Hook Block + Other Suspended Equipment

Utilization (%) = (Gross Load ÷ Rated Capacity) × 100

Remaining Capacity = Rated Capacity − Gross Load

Example

Main load = 40 t

Rigging = 2 t

Hook block = 5 t

Other suspended equipment = 1 t

Rated capacity = 60 t

Gross load = 40 + 2 + 5 + 1 = 48 t

Utilization = 48 ÷ 60 × 100 = 80%

Remaining capacity = 60 − 48 = 12 t

Important Superlift Concept

A superlift system can increase the crane's lifting capability by changing the crane's operating configuration and counterweight arrangement.

The actual benefit depends on the specific crane model, boom configuration, superlift system, counterweight, radius, boom angle, reeving and manufacturer load chart.

Critical Safety Notice

This calculator is for study and preliminary understanding only. It does not calculate or approve a crane's actual superlift capacity.

Do not use the calculated percentage as permission to perform a lift. Actual capacity must be verified using the specific manufacturer's load chart and configuration data.

Actual lifting operations require an approved lift plan, competent person verification, applicable standards, manufacturer requirements, site procedures and required inspections.
