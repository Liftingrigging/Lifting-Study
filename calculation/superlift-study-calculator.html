Important safety note: This calculator performs the arithmetic from the supplied reference image. It does not establish whether a crane or superlift configuration is safe for a lift. Verify the result against the exact crane manufacturer's approved load charts, superlift configuration, counterweight arrangement, reeving, and lift plan. Confirm whether the load weight must include rigging and lifting accessories. Do not use this result alone to authorize a lift.
