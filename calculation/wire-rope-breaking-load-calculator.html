Wire Rope Study

This calculator uses manufacturer-provided minimum breaking force (MBL/MBF) to study a simple design-factor relationship.

Basic Study Formula

Study Reference Load = Minimum Breaking Force ÷ Study Design Factor

Utilization (%) = Applied Load ÷ Study Reference Load × 100

Example:

MBF = 200 kN

Design factor = 5

Study reference load = 200 ÷ 5 = 40 kN

Important Wire Rope Factors

  • Rope diameter
  • Rope construction
  • Rope grade
  • Core type
  • Manufacturer
  • End termination
  • Bending and D/d ratio
  • Sheave and drum condition
  • Fleet angle
  • Corrosion and mechanical damage
  • Lubrication and maintenance
  • Rope reeving arrangement
Important Safety Notice

There is no single universal breaking-load formula that should be used to determine the actual lifting capacity of every wire rope.

Use the actual manufacturer's certified minimum breaking force, rope specification, termination requirements and applicable standard.

The design factor entered here is only a study input. It does not establish the legally or technically required factor for a particular lifting application.

Do not use this calculator to approve a crane rope, hoist rope, guy rope or lifting application. Actual suitability requires the manufacturer's data, applicable standards and competent-person verification.
