Important Unit Note

Mass and force are not the same physical quantity.

For example, 10 tonnes is a mass, while approximately 98.0665 kN is the gravitational force corresponding to that mass using standard gravity.

Always check whether a manufacturer, crane load chart, rigging certificate or engineering document specifies mass, force, WLL, SWL or another measurement.
Study and Safety Notice

This converter performs mathematical unit conversions. It does not determine lifting capacity, WLL, SWL, crane capacity or equipment suitability.

For lifting operations, use the units and values specified by the applicable manufacturer documentation, certificates, standards, approved lift plan and competent person.

Do not convert a manufacturer's rated value and assume that the converted number is automatically valid for a different lifting configuration.
