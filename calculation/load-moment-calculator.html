Study Formula

Load Moment = Load × Radius M = W × R

Where:

  • M = load moment
  • W = load
  • R = horizontal operating radius

When load is entered in tonnes and radius in metres, the result is expressed as tonne-metres (t·m).

Example Study Calculation

Load = 20 t Radius = 15 m M = 20 × 15 M = 300 t·m

Important Crane Capacity Note

Load moment is useful for understanding the relationship between load and radius, but it must not be used as a replacement for the crane manufacturer's load chart.

Two cranes can have similar theoretical load moments but different rated capacities because of their configuration and structural limitations.

Important Safety Notice

This calculator is intended for study and reference.

Actual crane capacity must be determined from the manufacturer's load chart for the exact crane, configuration, boom length, radius, counterweight, outrigger configuration, reeving and other applicable conditions.

Do not approve or plan an actual lift using load moment alone. Follow the approved lift plan and competent-person requirements.
