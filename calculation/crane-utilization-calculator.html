Study Formulas

Gross Load = Lifted Load + Rigging Weight + Hook Block Weight + Other Suspended Weight
Crane Utilization (%) = (Gross Load ÷ Rated Capacity) × 100

The rated capacity must represent the crane's actual configuration and operating condition at the applicable radius and boom configuration.

Example Study

Lifted load = 18 t

Rigging = 0.8 t

Hook block = 1.2 t

Other equipment = 0.5 t

Crane rated capacity = 30 t

Gross Load = 18 + 0.8 + 1.2 + 0.5 Gross Load = 20.5 tonnes

Utilization = (20.5 ÷ 30) × 100 Utilization = 68.3%

Interpretation

The percentage shown by this calculator is a study comparison only.

A lower percentage does not automatically mean that a lift is safe or approved.

Actual crane capacity depends on the manufacturer's load chart and the complete crane configuration, including radius, boom length, counterweight, outriggers, jib, reeving and other applicable factors.

Important Safety Notice

This calculator must not be used to select or approve a crane for an actual lifting operation.

The crane's rated capacity must be taken from the manufacturer's applicable load chart for the exact configuration and operating conditions.

Always use the approved lift plan, manufacturer's documentation, site requirements and competent-person verification.
