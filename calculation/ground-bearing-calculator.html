Calculate Ground Bearing Pressure

Enter the applied force and supporting area to calculate the average pressure on the ground.

P = F ÷ A

P = Ground Pressure (kN/m²)
F = Applied Force (kN)
A = Supporting Area (m²)
Enter force and support area.

Calculate Required Support Area

Enter the applied force and allowable ground pressure to estimate the minimum theoretical support area.

A = F ÷ Pallow

A = Required Support Area
F = Applied Force
Pallow = Allowable Ground Pressure
Enter force and allowable ground pressure.
📚 Study Concept

Increasing the supporting area reduces the average pressure applied to the ground, assuming the applied force remains constant.

For crane operations, actual outrigger reactions can vary significantly depending on crane configuration, load radius, boom configuration and operating conditions.

⚠️ Important Safety Notice

These calculations are simplified educational calculations. They do not determine whether actual ground conditions are suitable for a crane.

Actual crane setup requires verified outrigger reactions, ground investigation, allowable bearing capacity, support system design and competent engineering review.
