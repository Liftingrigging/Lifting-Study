Formula
Load force (kN) = outrigger reaction (tonnes) × 9.80665
Mat area (m²) = mat length × mat width
Ground pressure (kPa) = load force (kN) ÷ mat area (m²)
Required area (m²) = load force (kN) ÷ allowable pressure (kPa)
Engineering limitation: This calculator estimates average pressure assuming the full mat area is in effective contact with the ground and the load is spread uniformly. It does not check mat bending or shear strength, settlement, uneven support, slope stability, nearby excavations, buried services, or crane stability. A pressure result within the entered limit is not by itself approval to park the crane or perform a lift. Verify the crane reaction, effective bearing area, mat design and ground capacity with a competent engineer.
