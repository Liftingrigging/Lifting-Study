Formulas used
Reaction force (kN) = outrigger reaction (tonnes) × 9.80665
Mat area (m²) = mat length × mat width
Ground pressure (kPa) = reaction force (kN) ÷ mat area (m²)
Required area (m²) = reaction force (kN) ÷ allowable ground pressure (kPa)
Float pressure (kPa) = reaction force (kN) ÷ float contact area (m²)
Engineering limitations: This calculator checks average pressure using the entered reaction and assumed full rectangular mat contact area. It does not calculate the crane's outrigger reaction, mat bending or shear strength, load-spreading through the mat, ground settlement, slope stability, or failure near excavations and buried services. Passing this pressure check does not by itself approve crane parking or a lift.
Use of guidance: Use the manufacturer's maximum support reaction and a project-specific allowable ground pressure. The effective bearing area may be less than the mat's plan area if the mat is flexible, unevenly supported, damaged, or not fully in contact with the ground. Verify the mat and the supporting ground separately with a competent engineer.
