Calculation formula
Load force (kN) = reaction (tonnes) × 9.80665
Mat area (m²) = length × width
Ground pressure (kPa) = load force ÷ mat area
Required area (m²) = load force ÷ allowable pressure
Engineering safety note: This tool calculates average ground pressure only. It assumes the full mat area is effectively supported and the load is distributed uniformly. It does not check mat bending or shear strength, settlement, slope stability, nearby excavations, buried services, or crane stability. A result within the entered pressure limit does not by itself approve crane parking. Verify crane reactions, mat design, effective bearing area and ground capacity with a competent engineer. This is not an official TÜV NORD or LEEA calculator or certification.
All calculations update automatically. Units: tonnes, metres, kN, m² and kPa. Use manufacturer data and the project-specific ground assessment for actual lifting operations.
