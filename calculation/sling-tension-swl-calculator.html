4. Formulas used

Option 1 — Required SWL per sling
SWL = Load Weight ÷ (Hitch Value × Angle Factor)

Option 2 — Estimated total liftable load
Load Weight = SWL per sling × Number of Slings × Hitch Value × Angle Factor

Option 2 extends your formula to account for the selected number of slings, assuming equal load sharing.

Safety notice

The factor defaults are illustrative and are not verified TÜV SHADI values. The correct convention for angle and hitch factors must come from your approved chart. Use the manufacturer's rated WLL for the complete assembly, consider unequal load sharing and centre of gravity, and have the lift checked by a competent person. Do not use this calculator as the sole basis for a lifting operation.
