Safety notice

Angle factors and hitch values depend on the approved load chart and how the terms are defined. The illustrative angle factors in this page are not verified TÜV SHADI values. Confirm the factor table and manufacturer's instructions before lifting. The calculated required SWL is not the same as the rated capacity of a sling assembly. Never use this calculator alone to approve a lift.
