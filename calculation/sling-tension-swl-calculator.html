Safety notice

The angle factors and hitch values in this calculator are illustrative reference defaults, not verified TÜV SHADI factors. Option 1 estimates required capacity using the requested formula; Option 2 estimates liftable load using its inverse. Neither result is an approved lifting rating. Check the complete assembly's marked WLL and approved load chart, and have the lift verified by a competent person. Do not assume all sling legs share the load equally.
