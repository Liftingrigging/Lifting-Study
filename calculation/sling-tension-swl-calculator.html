For chain slings, use the rated single-leg capacity and the applicable manufacturer-approved angle and hitch factors. Do not calculate a certified WLL from chain diameter alone.

Use the factor from the applicable chain sling load chart.

Chain sling capacity formula:
Chain Sling Capacity = Rated Single-Leg Capacity × Angle Factor × Hitch Factor

For multiple legs, only apply a multi-leg rating or leg multiplier if it is explicitly permitted by the manufacturer's rated capacity chart and applicable standard.

Important Safety Information

Important: This is a planning aid, not a sling certification tool. Actual SWL/WLL must be based on the sling's identification tag, manufacturer's load chart, hitch arrangement, sling angle convention, number of effective load-bearing legs, connection hardware, and applicable lifting standards. Factors shown as examples are not universal. Verify all factors before using the result for a real lift. Never exceed the manufacturer's rated capacity.
