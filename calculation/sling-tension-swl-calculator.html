Formula used

Angle factor = sin(angle measured above horizontal)
Estimated capacity per leg = base WLL × hitch factor × angle factor
Illustrative total capacity = estimated capacity per leg × number of legs

The total-capacity result is a simplified illustration, not an approved multi-leg sling rating. Real multi-leg capacity can depend on sling assembly rating, included angles, load distribution, centre of gravity, unequal leg loading and the manufacturer's rated configuration.

Safety notice

Do not use this illustrative calculator as the sole basis for a lifting operation. Use the manufacturer's WLL tag and approved load chart for the exact sling assembly and hitch. Do not exceed the rated capacity. Confirm the load's centre of gravity, sling angles, attachment points, condition of equipment and applicable site lifting procedure with a competent person.
