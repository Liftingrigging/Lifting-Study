Lift Planning Workspace

Check simplified crane geometry, estimate the boom length needed to clear a building, and prepare your lift checklist.

1. Crane Geometry Calculator

Enter any two values to calculate the third for an idealized straight boom. This does not replace a crane manufacturer's range diagram.

2. Obstacle Clearance Calculator

Enter the building's near-edge distance, building length, roof height, and boom geometry. The results show only vertical and horizontal clearance. Positive horizontal clearance means the boom reaches the roof height before the building's near edge.

3. Load Placement Calculator

Estimate the straight-line boom length and angle required to reach beyond a building while keeping the boom centreline above its roof. The radius is measured horizontally from the crane's rotation centre to the building's near edge; building length extends from that edge to the far edge.

Important Safety Note

These tools provide simplified geometric estimates only. The boom centreline is not the same as the full boom envelope, and actual boom configuration, deflection, jib arrangement, crane orientation, load movement, and manufacturer-specific geometry can change the required clearances.

Before lifting, verify the complete lift path, required clearance, crane capacity, and configuration against the manufacturer's documents and an approved lift plan reviewed by a competent lifting professional.
