Lift Planning Tools

Use these tools for preliminary geometry checks and lift-planning calculations. Verify all dimensions, crane configuration, manufacturer load charts and site conditions before carrying out a lift.

1. Crane Geometry Calculator

Select two different known values, enter them, then select the value to calculate.

2. Obstacle Clearance Calculator

Estimates boom-centreline clearance over a straight building footprint. Enter all heights and lengths in metres and the boom angle in degrees.

Preliminary geometric estimate only. This is not a manufacturer-approved crane calculation, lifting study or regulatory clearance assessment. Confirm the actual boom geometry, crane configuration, load path, deflection, tolerances and site conditions with the responsible lifting engineer and approved lift plan.
